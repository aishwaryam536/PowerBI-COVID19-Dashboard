## Key Insights

### 1. Global COVID-19 Cases

The dashboards provide an overview of confirmed COVID-19 cases, deaths, recovered cases, and active cases across different continents and countries.

### 2. Continent-Wise Analysis

The continent-wise dashboard compares daily new cases, daily new deaths, active cases, and recovered cases across Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, South America, and Australia/Oceania.

### 3. Country-Wise Distribution

The country-wise dashboard shows the geographic distribution of COVID-19 activity across countries using a map and country-level comparisons.

### 4. Confirmed, Recovered, Active Cases and Deaths

The dashboards provide separate KPI and gauge visuals for confirmed cases, recovered cases, active cases, and deaths, allowing the overall status of the pandemic to be monitored.

### 5. COVID-19 Recovery

The country-level analysis compares total recovered cases across countries, with the dashboard showing higher recovery totals for countries such as the USA, India, Brazil, Russia, and the UK.

### 6. Daily and Time-Based Trends

Daily new cases and deaths are analyzed over time. The summary dashboard also provides monthly and yearly views to observe changes in COVID-19 activity.

### 7. Testing and Population

The summary report includes total population and total testing metrics, providing additional context for understanding the scale of COVID-19 activity.

### 8. Geographic Analysis

The map visual provides an interactive geographic view of COVID-19 data, allowing users to explore differences between countries.

### 9. Interactive Analysis

Continent and country filters allow users to interactively explore the COVID-19 data and compare different geographic areas.

## Overall Insight

The Power BI dashboards provide a consolidated view of COVID-19 cases, deaths, recoveries, active cases, testing, population, and trends across countries and continents. The interactive visuals make it easier to explore geographic and time-based patterns in the dataset.
